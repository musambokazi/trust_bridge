# Business evidence and remaining validation

SABRIC's release of its 2025 Annual Banking Crime Statistics reports digital banking losses of R2.4 billion in 2025. Source: https://www.sabric.co.za/sabric-release-the-2025-annual-banking-crime-statistics-report/ (accessed 2026-11-12). This is a broad digital-banking statistic, not a measurement of invoice fraud, SME losses, TrustBridge detection accuracy or prevented losses.

The lean canvas prices remain hypotheses: Starter R99/50 monthly assessments, Business R299/300, Professional R799/1,000. The app implements workspace usage metrics, server quotas and non-financial plan requests. New workspaces use a free 1,000-check pilot allowance. There is no live merchant checkout, recurring collection, tax invoice or refund flow. Do not claim paying customers or subscriptions from plan requests.

Validation protocol: recruit consenting SMEs/bookkeepers through authorised outreach; ask how supplier changes are currently verified, where evidence is kept, who can amend bank details, what a missed change costs, and which review steps are practical. Run matching, altered-bank, tax mismatch, missing-field and unknown-supplier examples using fictional data. Record task completion, false flags and user corrections. Ask willingness to pay without presenting proposed pricing as confirmed demand.

Measure real registrations, monthly active workspaces, comparisons, discrepancy tiers, review completion, reliability and retention using server aggregates. Accuracy requires labelled cases with independently established ground truth; discrepancy counts alone are not accuracy or fraud-prevention counts. Customer interviews, payments, distribution partnerships and six-month targets are not fabricated by implementation work.

Operator-only aggregate queries are in supabase/metrics.sql. Marketing channels, store distribution, partnerships and revenue forecasts require real execution and evidence after technical acceptance.
